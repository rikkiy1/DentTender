Services

Door Dings & Minor Dents

Those annoying door dings and dents don't have to be an eyesore, most of these can be fixed by PDR, and at a very reasonable cost. These repairs are generally a same day service, and most can be completed in a few hours or less.

Hail Damage

Hail damages are generally covered under your comprehensive insurance policy. PDR is an insurance company approved and preferred method of repair for hail damages. Even if you don't have hail coverage, it may still be worthwhile to have your hail damage fixed, as the depreciation in value will very likely be higher than the cost of repair. Depending on the extent of the damage, the repair may take 1 - 3 days.

Bumper Repair

For best results, plastic bumper damages should be repaired as soon as possible, usually within a week to avoid permanent crease marks.

Please note, we only repair dents in bumpers, and Do Not do paint touch-ups, or repair cracks.

Mobile Service

We are a mobile paintless dent repair service, so, we will come to you, and fix your dings and dents at a time and location convenient to YOU! So, you don't have to make time to bring your car to the shop and wait around for hours while your car is being serviced. We serve the Calgary and surrounding areas, and you can expect to receive service within a week. Please contact us to arrange an appointment.
